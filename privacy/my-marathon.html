Privacy policy

Sleeps Til My Marathon

Effective date: 14 November 2026

1. About this app

Sleeps Til My Marathon is a marathon countdown app and home-screen widget. It helps users count down to a selected marathon or race date and personalise the appearance of their widget, including its background, athlete artwork and motivational messages.

The app is designed as a countdown and motivation tool. It does not provide medical advice, training prescriptions or professional fitness guidance.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores information needed to operate its features, including:

  • Your selected marathon or event date.
  • Your widget appearance and personalisation preferences.
  • Other settings required for the countdown and widget.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to calculate and display your countdown.

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, precise location or contact details to use the app.

3. Information collected or shared by VJstuff

The app is designed to keep the selected event date and widget preferences on your device. VJstuff does not collect, receive, sell or share this app-entered information.

4. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app does not contain advertising and does not use analytics or cross-app tracking.

VJstuff does not use the app to build advertising profiles or track users across other apps or websites.

5. Device and operating-system services

The app uses Android's widget and system scheduling features to keep the countdown updated, including after a device restart or a change to the device's date or time.

Android and your device manufacturer may provide their own backup or device services according to your system and account settings. These services are controlled by Android or your device provider, not by VJstuff.

6. Children

Sleeps Til My Marathon is a general-purpose countdown app and is not directed specifically to children. VJstuff does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

7. Security

The app's countdown date and preferences are stored in the app's local settings. Users should protect their devices using the security features available on them.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can change your event date and widget preferences within the app.

Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to any device-level backup or restore features you have enabled.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. If future versions introduce analytics, advertising, online services or other data handling, this policy and the applicable app-store disclosures will be updated accordingly.
