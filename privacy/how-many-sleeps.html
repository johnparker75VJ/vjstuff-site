How Many Sleeps Til I Retire?

Effective date: 24 October 2026

1. About this app

How Many Sleeps Til I Retire? is a retirement countdown app and home-screen widget. It calculates the number of sleeps remaining until a retirement date selected by the user and lets the user customise the widget's appearance and encouragement phrase.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the retirement target date and widget preferences in the app's local settings so it can calculate and display the countdown. The calculation and widget customisation are performed on your device.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal details to use its countdown features.

3. Information collected or shared by VJstuff

The reviewed app build does not include an internet permission or an analytics, advertising, authentication, or crash-reporting SDK. Based on that build, the app does not send the retirement date, widget settings, or other app-entered information to VJstuff, and VJstuff does not sell or share app-entered personal information.

4. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The reviewed app build does not include analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking functionality.

5. Device and operating-system services

The app uses Android's widget and system scheduling features to keep the widget updated, including after a device restart or a change to the device's date or time. Android and your device manufacturer may provide their own backup or device services according to your system and account settings. Those services are controlled by Android or your device provider, not by VJstuff.

6. Children

This app is a general-purpose retirement countdown and is not directed specifically to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

The app's countdown date and preferences are kept in the app's local settings. Keep your device protected with the security features available on it. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can change or clear the retirement date and widget preferences in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to any device-level backup or restore features you have enabled.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any future version that adds analytics, advertising, online services, or other data handling will require this policy and the applicable app-store disclosures to be updated before release.
