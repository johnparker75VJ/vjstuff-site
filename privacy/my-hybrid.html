Privacy Policy

Sleeps Til My Hybrid

Effective date: 14 November 2026

1. About this app

Sleeps Til My Hybrid is an Android countdown and motivation widget that helps users count down to a hybrid fitness event.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the event date you select, widget appearance preferences, athlete-image preferences and the information needed to display the countdown and motivational messages. This information is stored locally on your Android device.

3. Information collected or shared

VJstuff does not collect, receive or share personal information through Sleeps Til My Hybrid. The app does not require an account, and the current version does not transmit the information stored by the app to VJstuff or to another service.

4. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version does not include advertising, analytics or tracking technologies.

5. Third-party services

The current version does not integrate third-party services for accounts, cloud storage, synchronisation, advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

6. Permissions

The app uses the Android boot-completed permission so that its widget can refresh after the device restarts. It does not request access to contacts, location, photos, microphone or other personal information.

7. Children

Sleeps Til My Hybrid is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Data security and deletion

App preferences and countdown settings remain on your device. You can remove this locally stored information by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app. Because the current version does not send this information to VJstuff, VJstuff does not hold a separate copy to delete.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this policy was last revised.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact VJstuff at VJstuff@yahoo.com.
